PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS DOCUMENT
Project: EAI DealFlow Terminal (DFT)
Version: 2.0 ("The Modern UI Update")
Owner: Investment Team, EAI Capital
Development Lead: [Your Name]
Last Updated: January 2026

1. Executive Summary
The EAI DealFlow Terminal is a bespoke, AI-powered sourcing engine designed to identify, analyze, and contact off-market acquisition targets.
Moving away from legacy "Excel-style" tools, Version 2.0 adopts a "High-Finance SaaS" aesthetic (inspired by Stripe/Linear). It leverages proprietary transaction data to generate instant valuation benchmarks and automates the creation of hyper-personalized outreach campaigns.
Primary Objective: Reduce the "Time-to-Pitch" for analysts from 45 minutes to <60 seconds per lead, while increasing response rates via data-backed "Action Plans."

2. Design Language & User Experience (UX)
The application prioritizes a "Dark Glass" aesthetic to convey sophistication, speed, and precision.
2.1. Visual Identity
Theme: Modern Dark Mode (Obsidian & Slate).
Background: Deep Charcoal (#0E1117) with subtle radial gradients—no flat black.
Glassmorphism: Content containers use semi-transparent backgrounds (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)) with a 1px subtle border and backdrop blur to create depth.
Typography: Clean, premium Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter or SF Pro) for UI text; Monospace for financial data points only.
Color Palette:
Primary Action: Electric Blurple Gradient (Blue to Purple) for high-value buttons.
Data Accents: Cyan (#00E5FF) for "Your Target," Muted Grey (#6B7280) for "Market Peers."
Success: Emerald Green (#10B981) for positive signals.
2.2. Interaction Design
Rounded Corners: All buttons, inputs, and cards have border-radius: 12px (Modern standard).
Hover States: Subtle glow effects on interactive elements.
Feedback: Instant visual feedback (e.g., progress bars) during AI generation.

3. Functional Requirements
3.1. The Data Ingestion Engine (Backend)
Auto-Discovery: The system scans the local data/ directory on launch. It automatically detects and merges any new CSV files (HVAC.csv, Trucking.csv, etc.).
Normalization Layer:
Standardizes inconsistent headers (e.g., maps "SDE_Margin" and "SDE %" to a single SDE Margin column).
Cleans "dirty" financial data (strips $, ,, x, % symbols) for accurate math.
Peer Group Logic:
Dynamically filters the dataset based on the user's selected Industry and Revenue Range (+/- 50% variance) to ensure apples-to-apples comparisons.
3.2. The "Deal Heat" Algorithm (Sidebar)
A real-time scoring engine that rates the attractiveness of a lead before the analyst invests time.
Input: Target Revenue, Industry Quality, Data Completeness.
Scoring Logic (0-100 Scale):
Base Score: 50 points.
Revenue Match: +25 points if Revenue is within EAI's "Sweet Spot" ($2M - $10M).
Market Density: +15 points if >5 comparable peers exist in the database (high confidence data).
Margin Health: +10 points if the peer group median EBITDA margin is >15%.
Visual Output: A sleek, color-coded progress bar in the sidebar (Red < 50, Yellow 50-75, Green > 75).
3.3. Interactive Market Visualization (Main UI)
Technology: Plotly (Interactive).
Chart Type: "Galaxy" Scatter Plot.
Axes:
X-Axis: Revenue ($).
Y-Axis: EBITDA Margin (%).
Visuals:
Peers: Small, semi-transparent white dots (Ghost data).
Target: A large, pulsing Cyan orb representing the prospect's estimated position.
Interactivity: Hovering over any dot reveals the anonymized deal details (Date, Revenue, Multiple) of that comparable transaction.
3.4. The AI Communication Suite (Right Panel)
Uses Google Gemini 2.0 Flash to draft a 3-stage drip campaign.
Tab 1: The "Hook" (Initial Outreach)
Strategy: Operational Value Proposition.
Content: "We analyzed {Industry} data. Companies of your size usually trade at {Multiple}x. We see an operational gap that could increase this to {Target}x."
Tab 2: The "Asset" (Value Add)
Strategy: Reciprocity.
Content: "I prepared a one-page Valuation Snapshot specifically for {Company}. It benchmarks you against 15 recent sales. Attached below."
Tab 3: The "Close" (Professional Nudge)
Strategy: Low friction.
Content: "Any thoughts on the report? Happy to walk through the raw data for 5 minutes."
3.5. The "One-Pager" Generator (PDF)
A professionally designed, downloadable asset to attach to Email #2.
File Format: High-resolution PDF.
Design: Minimalist header, EAI Capital branding.
Sections:
Executive Summary: "Valuation Estimate for [Company Name]."
Market Comp Table: Top 3 most similar transactions (Anonymized).
The "Golden Graph": A static version of the scatter plot showing their position.
Operational Upside: 3 bullet points on potential value creation.

4. Technical Architecture
4.1. Stack
Language: Python 3.12+
Framework: Streamlit (v1.30+)
AI Model: gemini-2.0-flash (via google-genai client).
Visualization: plotly.graph_objects
Reporting: fpdf2
4.2. Security & Compliance
API Keys: Handled via environment variables or secure input; never hardcoded in shared repos.
Data Privacy: Local CSV processing only. No proprietary deal data is sent to the AI model (only aggregated metrics/prompts are sent).

5. User Flow (The "Happy Path")
Analyst Launch: User opens the terminal. Sidebar loads with "Ready" state.
Input: Analyst types "Bob's HVAC", pastes "www.bobshvac.com", selects "HVAC", and inputs "$4,500,000" Revenue.
Analysis:
System calculates "Deal Heat: 85/100" (High Quality).
Main chart renders, placing Bob's HVAC in the top-right quadrant (High Revenue, High Margin potential).
Strategy Generation:
Analyst reviews "Email 1" (The Hook). AI has correctly referenced the 4.5x multiple.
Analyst clicks "Generate Report". System builds Bobs_HVAC_Valuation.pdf.
Execution: Analyst copies Email 1 to Outlook, attaches the PDF to the draft for Email 2, and logs the interaction.

6. Future Roadmap (v3.0)
LinkedIn Integration: Auto-fetch owner name via URL scraping.
CRM Sync: One-click push to HubSpot/Salesforce.
Sentiment Analysis: AI auto-reads replies to categorize them (Interested vs. Unsubscribe).
